The brief: Mashover’s support team could not keep up. Slow replies were costing sales, and hiring more agents was too expensive for a clothing brand at their volume.

Problem

Support volume outgrew the team — peak hours meant lost carts and unhappy buyers.

  • 120+ daily inquiries drowning the inbox
  • 2.5-hour average response time
  • Churn from slow, uneven service
  • Staffing more agents was not viable
  • Sales leaking during peak hours
→

Solution

A 24/7 AI assistant that answers from the catalog, CRM, and knowledge base — then hands off when a human is needed.

  • NLP chatbot trained on Mashover’s catalog
  • CRM, knowledge base, and product sync
  • 58% of inquiries closed automatically
  • Smart escalation to live agents
  • Sentiment, lead qualification, tickets
→

Impact

Routine questions never wait. The team now spends time on returns, VIP orders, and the cases that actually need a person.

  • 120+ inquiries handled every day
  • 45-second typical first response
  • 52% stronger sales conversion
  • $95K annual support cost saved
  • Satisfaction up from 3.2 to 4.1 / 5
